# Data Analytics - Level 1 - Task 1
## EDA On Retail Sales

### Objective
To perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on retail store sales data and identify useful sales, customer, and product insights.

### Dataset
Retail Sales Dataset containing information about sales transactions, customers, products, categories, age, gender, and sales amounts.

### Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

### What I Did

#### 1. Data Loading and Initial Inspection
- Loaded the retail sales dataset using Pandas.
- Checked the dataset shape.
- Checked data types.
- Checked for missing/null values.
- Inspected the dataset structure.

#### 2. Descriptive Statistics
Calculated:
- Mean
- Median
- Mode
- Standard Deviation

for the numerical columns.

#### 3. Sales Trend Analysis
- Analyzed monthly sales trends using line charts.
- Analyzed quarterly sales trends to understand sales patterns over time.

#### 4. Customer Demographics
Analyzed:
- Age groups
- Gender distribution
- Sales patterns across different customer groups

#### 5. Product Analysis
- Identified the Top 10 best-selling products.
- Analyzed revenue generated by different product categories.

#### 6. Correlation Analysis
Created a correlation heatmap to understand relationships between numerical variables.

#### 7. Additional Visualization
Created additional visualizations to identify patterns and insights that were not immediately visible from the basic analysis.

### Key Insights
1. Sales vary across different months and quarters, helping identify periods of higher and lower sales activity.
2. Certain product categories contribute more revenue than others.
3. Different age groups and genders show different purchasing patterns.
4. Top-selling products contribute significantly to overall sales.
5. Correlation analysis helps identify relationships between numerical variables.

### Business Recommendations
1. Focus marketing campaigns and promotional offers during high-performing sales periods.
2. Maintain sufficient inventory for high-demand products and categories.
3. Use customer demographic insights to create targeted marketing campaigns.
4. Promote high-performing products while identifying opportunities to improve lower-performing categories.

### Conclusion
The EDA provides insights into sales trends, customer demographics, product performance, and relationships between numerical variables. These findings can help businesses understand customer behavior, monitor sales performance, and make data-driven decisions.
